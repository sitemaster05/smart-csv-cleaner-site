Personal Job Assistant: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026.

This Google OAuth application ("jobs") is a private tool used only by its owner, Aleksandr Kim, on his own computer. It is not offered to the public.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent the application reads the owner's own Gmail messages through the read-only scope gmail.readonly. It cannot send, change or delete email.

How data is used

It finds job alerts and platform invitations and shows them to the owner. Text from job-related messages may be sent to the Google Gemini API to summarise an opportunity or draft a reply. Results are stored locally on the owner's computer.

Sharing

Data is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with anyone else. Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Removal

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; local data can then be deleted by the owner.
