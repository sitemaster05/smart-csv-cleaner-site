Privacy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

The app

Smart CSV Cleaner Pro works entirely on your computer. It has no account, no telemetry, no analytics and no network code: your CSV files, reports and cleaning profiles never leave your device. Settings and profiles are stored in %LOCALAPPDATA%\SmartCSVCleanerPro and can be deleted at any time.

This website

This page uses no cookies, trackers or analytics. The browser demo cleans the sample text inside your browser and does not send it anywhere.

Purchases

When you buy a license, Lemon Squeezy processes your order as the merchant of record and receives the details needed for payment, tax and delivery (such as your name, email address, country and payment information). Their handling of that data is described in the Lemon Squeezy privacy policy. The developer sees your order details (name, email, country, product) in order to provide support and refunds and does not sell or share them.
